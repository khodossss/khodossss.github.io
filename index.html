  • Secured the production server: firewall open to SSH only, fail2ban, origin reachable only through Cloudflare, CSP and HSTS headers, nightly off-site backups to Cloudflare R2, and dropped privileges with memory limits for the backend, bots and the AI analytics sandbox.
